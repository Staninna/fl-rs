# Todo

The next big steps, biggest payoff first. None of them needs FL Studio running: each is checked against FL's DLLs or against this project's own output.

1. **Run the heavy instruments' voices in parallel.** The projects that drop out are mostly one instrument doing all the work: JuiceBass - SeamlessR - Menagerie is 78% one Harmor, and Olbaid - Harmless Essentials is nearly all one Harmless.
   - Done: Harmor, Harmless and Sytrus render their voices on the mixer's workers (`plugins::workers`, `Generator::set_workers`, the board in `crates/engine/src/pool.rs`), each voice into a buffer of its own, summed in the voice order one thread uses, so all 165 profile-harness hashes are unchanged. A plugin playing on a worker in the middle of the mixer's round shares its voices too: whoever is free joins, and nobody waits for a call that has not started. `FL_RS_THREADS=1` renders as before, at the same cost (within 0.6% of the instructions). Sytrus renders on one thread in a block where a voice starts, an operator is plucked, or a shared unison's master is released, since those touch what its voices share.
   - Left: what the voices feed is still one thread. Harmless Essentials is about 45% voices and 45% its reverb and the Maximus behind them, so four threads take it only from 0.6x to 1.0x on a busy machine (Menagerie 1.2x to 1.7x, Oxygen 0.8x to 1.5x, `.scratch/handoff.md`); Harmor's and Harmless's effect chains (Maximus's saturator `pow`, Fruity Reverb) are the next limit, and running a chunk's effects while the workers render the next chunk's voices would overlap them without changing a bit. Generators inside a Patcher get no workers yet (`Told` would carry them). Sytrus with plucked operators stays on one thread: the strings are one pool the voices take from as they play.

2. **Take the work out of the first play.** The profile harness's cold round shows where pressing play drops out: Bombs Away ft KARRA - I'm Awake's slowest cold call is 1339% of its budget against 485% once warm, and Right Night feeling's is 751% against 147%. The harness's cold columns show whether it worked.
   - Done, per plugin: the plugin bench times each plugin's cold first call (the `cold` columns), and the plugins whose first call did setup now do it at load, with the same output to the bit. Gross Beat cleared its 25 MB ring on the first play, which touched every page of it (8.4 ms, 73% of a call, to 16 µs); it now leaves a ring that holds only zeros alone. GMS built its ladder table in its first block (0.72 ms to 0.25 ms) and now builds it when made. Pitcher left a restart its parameters ask for to its first block and now does it at load. Every other plugin's first call runs the same instructions as a later call with the same notes (checked under callgrind); what is left of their cold time is cold caches and first-touched pages, and Ogun's and Autogun's is the work of their first notes.
   - Left, in the engine: rerun the profile harness on the corpus to see what the cold rounds of Bombs Away and Right Night feeling are now, and find what else is cold there (the mixer, the engine's own buffers, plugins on their project states rather than their defaults). A silent block at load would advance LFOs and envelopes, so it changes the output and is not the way: move setup, not processing.
